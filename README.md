# SOLMARIS — Sun · Streets · Score

A complete, original open-world crime game that plays offline in a desktop browser from its own files. An entry for [make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01).

Solmaris is a sun-soaked coastal city, and everybody in it owes somebody something. You are **Riko Vale**, a driver for hire with a fast car and a faster reputation. Your fixer **Mara** works the marina — eight jobs stand between you and the big score, and the Vultures want the harbour for themselves. Recruit a **crew**, earn each district's **standing**, race the streets, and hunt the hidden **sun charms**. Drive clean or drive loud. Just don't get caught.

## What is in the game

- **A city to explore on foot and by car**: a 7×7 street grid with ~80 buildings, a park, a parking lot with parked cars, a beach, a marina with a pier and boats, palms and streetlights, drifting clouds, zebra crossings at every intersection, and a sun that follows you.
- **A living day**: the clock runs from morning into golden hour and dusk — the sun drops, the sky warms, the shadows stretch. Six named districts (Downtown, The Marina, Northside, West Docks, East Alley, The Beach) show on the HUD as you cross them.
- **Traffic and pedestrians**: 22 cars driving the streets (they turn at intersections and brake for the car ahead), 12 parked cars plus a full parking lot, and 38 pedestrians who stroll the sidewalks and scatter when trouble starts.
- **Cars to steal and drive**: sedans, taxis, sports cars and an armoured truck — arcade handling with handbrake drifts, damage, smoke and explosions. Jacking a moving car is a hold: the driver brakes, Riko works the door, the driver bails and flees, and the law notices if a unit is close.
- **A wanted level and police who respond**: 1–5 stars for stealing, hitting and shooting in public; police cars roll in along the street grid, hunt you down and try to box you in. At 3 stars **Air One**, the police helicopter, joins the chase with a searchlight, and units set **roadblocks** across the road ahead. The **dispatch board** names every responding unit — callsign, distance, and its role (RAMMING, FLANKING, FOOT CHASE, CLOSING IN, RESPONDING, ROADBLOCK, SPOTLIGHT) — plus the **crime** on the charge line and a live **radio ticker** of the pursuit. The IN SIGHT / HIDDEN indicator tells you when you're clear. Break line of sight to cool off — or get busted. Or pay $200 at the **spray garage** to repaint the car and wipe the heat.
- **Missions and a story**: eight jobs for Mara — First Fare, Getaway, Harbor Heat, The Exchange, Night Shift, Rat Trap, Bank Job, The Big Score — in three acts (SMALL TIME, HEAT, KINGPIN), with briefings, dialogue cards, beacons, checkpoints, timers, shootouts, escapes and a story-complete ending. Every mission earns a **Gold / Silver / Bronze rating** against its par time.
- **A crew with abilities**: three specialists join as the story lands — **Nadia "Ghost" Rey** the driver (CLEAN PLATES, **Z**: drop a wanted star), **Iris "Eyes" Voss** the lookout (OVERWATCH, **C**: mark every cop and Vulture on the minimap for 20s), and **Theo "Fix" Marlowe** the mechanic (FIELD REPAIR, **X**: repair the car to full on the spot). Each has a real cooldown, shown on the crew cards.
- **District standing**: every neighbourhood rates Riko 0–100, from HOSTILE to BELOVED. Missions, fares and courier jobs raise it; friendly streets hide you (heat decays twice as fast, and the locals get the credit), hostile streets bring the law 15% closer.
- **Street races**: four time trials — Marina Mile, Downtown Circuit, Beach Run, Northside Climb — from cyan R markers. 3-2-1-GO, checkpoint beacons, a route line on the minimap, a live race HUD (checkpoint, timer, best, Gold target), and a medal that pays cash and respect. The police do not pause for a race.
- **Sun charms and stunt jumps**: 20 hidden golden charms at ground level around the city (no minimap blips — finding them is the hunt), and 6 stunt ramps that launch the car on a real ballistic arc. Land more than 15m out and it pays $10 a metre; the longest jump is a career stat.
- **A career to complete**: cars stolen, cops escaped, kilometres driven, people taken out, longest jump, abilities used, times the locals hid you, fares run, race golds, charms found, mission golds — and a **completion percentage** across seven buckets (story, race golds, charms, stunts, friendly districts, crew, gold ratings), all on the pause menu's STATS tab.
- **A pause menu with tabs**: MAP (big city map with district standing, markers, and click-to-set **waypoint**), STORY (acts, missions, ratings, rewards), STATS (completion ring, checklist, medals, rank, career grid), CREW, and SAVE. Q/E switch tabs, M jumps straight to the map.
- **Three save slots**: each with percent, missions, location, money and date on the title screen's slot cards and the pause menu's SAVE tab. Autosave writes the active slot.
- **District jobs**: one courier run per district — pick up the package at the orange J marker, deliver it across town for $250 and +8 standing in the district that sent it. Jobs cycle through all six districts.
- **An arsenal and a weapon wheel**: six weapons — Fists, the SPARROW 9 pistol, the HORNET SMG, the BULLDOG shotgun, the LONGSHOT rifle and the **MAULER** RPG (KINGPIN only: one rocket, 150 damage, and a 6m explosion that flattens everything nearby) — each with its own damage, range, magazine and reload. Buy them at **Harbor Arms** (ammo, vests and medkits too) with cash and respect; swap with **1–6** or open the radial wheel with **Q**.
- **Respect**: every job, fare, carjack and clean getaway earns respect. Rise from NOBODY through RUNNER, HUSTLER and BIG SHOT to KINGPIN — and better guns, garage tiers and the Big Score itself open up along the way.
- **A garage that tunes cars**: **North Garage** sells engine tiers, armour tiers, a turbo and resprays — upgrades live on the car, and the tuned car feels it: faster, tougher, and marked with a star on the vehicle panel.
- **A safehouse**: rest for free (full health and armour), save the game, and park one car in the garage — its paint and upgrades come back exactly as you left them.
- **Side work between jobs**: taxi fares — pull up to a waiting fare, drive them across town, get paid by the distance, and the destination district warms to you. Health and armour pickups at the hospital, police HQ, marina, bank, safehouse and garage (armour had no way to be earned before).
- **Combat**: tracers and muzzle flash, fists, armed Vultures with health bars who shoot back, and health that regens after a quiet stretch.
- **A HUD with a minimap**: wanted stars, money, clock, radio station and track, district name and standing bar, mission objective with distance (act label, race timer, waypoint distance), health/armour bars, speed, a vehicle panel (model, condition %, gear), crew cards with cooldowns, the dispatch board, and a live round minimap with the mission marker, race route, waypoint, police blips, the helicopter, pickups, enemies and shop icons.
- **A start screen and saves**: title menu with story intro, three save-slot cards and controls, pause menu, autosave to localStorage, Continue on return.
- **Synthesized sound**: engine hum, sirens, gunshots, crashes and three radio stations (SOL FM, VOLTAGE, MARINA JAZZ — switch with N), each with three named tracks that rotate every 90 seconds, all made live with WebAudio — no audio files, no network.
- **Original everything**: own city, characters, vehicles, names, story, art and sound. No Rockstar / GTA content. Three.js (r160, minified build) is vendored under `game/vendor/`.

## Play it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

Controls: **WASD** move / drive · **Mouse** camera · **Click** shoot · **F** punch · **E** enter/exit car, talk, pick up, open shops, start race · **1–6 / Q** weapons / wheel · **Z / X / C** crew abilities · **Shift** run · **Space** jump / handbrake · **R** reload · **N** radio · **P** pause · **M** map.

## Hand it over

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
# writes out/game/, out/screenshots/ and out/dossier.json
```

## Layout

- `game/` — the playable game: `index.html` and `src/` (one file per system: `config`, `utils`, `textures`, `audio`, `input`, `city`, `effects`, `vehicles`, `pedestrians`, `player`, `police`, `helicopter`, `combat`, `missions`, `weapons`, `respect`, `crew`, `standing`, `races`, `collectibles`, `stunts`, `career`, `pause`, `garage`, `shops`, `pickups`, `activities`, `hud`, `save`, `shots`, `main`), plus vendored `vendor/three.module.min.js`.
- `screenshots/` — 4 real renders of the game's own code: the golden-hour getaway chase with the helicopter (cover), the dusk marina shootout, the Marina Mile street race, and the pause menu's career page.
- `main.ts` — the handover program; `vendor/makefun-game.ts` is the kit's file, unchanged.
