Solmaris is a sun-soaked coastal city, and everybody in it owes somebody something. You are Riko Vale, a driver for hire with a fast car and a faster reputation. Your fixer Mara works the marina — eight jobs stand between you and the big score, and the Vultures want the harbour for themselves. Earn respect to unlock iron at Harbor Arms, tune your ride at the North Garage, and park the good ones at your safehouse. Drive clean or drive loud. Just don't get caught.
